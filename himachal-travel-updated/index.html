Frequently asked questions

Can I customise my trip?

Yes. Share your preferred destinations, dates and budget in Special requests. We will discuss a tailored itinerary before confirmation.

When is my booking confirmed?

After the operator checks availability and accepts your request. Save your booking reference and check Track booking using your original email.

How do I pay?

After agreeing the final quote and terms, use the secure payment link if available. Never share your card details in chat.

What if I need to cancel?

Contact the operator. Cancellation and refund terms must be agreed in writing before payment.

How do I contact my driver or hotel?

Once the operator adds travel details, confirmed guests can find them in Track booking and download a confirmation PDF.
